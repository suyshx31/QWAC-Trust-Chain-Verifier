eIDAS 2.0 · QWAC Trust Chain Demonstrator

Tracing certificate provenance for EU banking onboarding

This tool resolves a bank's domain against publicly logged Certificate Transparency records and cross-references the issuing authority with the EU's official register of Qualified Trust Service Providers. Every data point shown is fetched live from authoritative, verifiable sources.
